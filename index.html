About Me

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Mechanical Engineering with the PEY Co-Op program at the University of Toronto (Sept 2024 – May 2029 expected), maintaining a 3.61/4.0 GPA with 4x Dean's Honours List distinctions.

My hands-on industry exposure spans mechanical design at Nokia, manufacturing and fabrication at Oasis Metal Manufacturing, and heavy vehicle systems at Al Shirawi Enterprises across Chennai and Dubai. On campus, I serve in multiple Engineering Society leadership capacities — including Class Rep, Discipline Rep, Ombudsperson, and rep on the Undergraduate Assessment Committee — as well as Hull & Structural Executive on the U of T Concrete Canoe Design Team.

"I believe in designing practical, manufacturable solutions that balance performance, cost, and reliability — grounded in robust design software and real-world shop-floor experience."
